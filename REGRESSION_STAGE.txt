CARRERA-HUB REGRESSION STAGE 4R3
================================
Name: Recovery Delay Hardening (30s Minimum)

Baseline: Stage 3B - Startup Full Terminal Reset
Parent experiment: Stage 4R2

Changes:
- Preserves all Stage 4R2 package-isolated recovery behavior.
- Adds configurable `RECOVERY_DELAY_SECONDS`, defaulting to 30 seconds.
- Enforces a hard minimum of 30 seconds even if configuration is lower.
- Single-package recovery waits the full delay after crash/force-close detection before starting recovery tiers.
- The existing package-local PID-release check remains after the delay.
- Global recovery delay behavior is unchanged.
- Dashboard rendering and the one-time startup full terminal reset are unchanged.

Test focus:
1. Confirm a single-package force close waits at least 30 seconds before recovery launch.
2. Force-close clienu while clienv remains healthy.
3. Force-close clienv while clienu remains healthy.
4. Force-close both packages and confirm each respects its own 30-second delay.
5. Repeat with 5-7 packages and confirm no recovery stampede.
6. Verify a failed target remains RECOVER/FAILED and never becomes Farming.

This stage is experimental and is NOT the baseline until user testing passes.
